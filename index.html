How It Works

1

AI Generates Fair Lines

Before every game, our models ingest team data, injuries, form, venue, travel, and market context — then output an independent fair line for each market.

2

Thousands of Simulations Run

The simulation engine runs every game thousands of times, producing score distributions, win probabilities, and expected value surfaces across spread, moneyline, and total markets.

3

Edges Are Surfaced

Our system compares fair lines and simulation outputs against live sportsbook odds. When a meaningful gap exists, it's highlighted — no handicapper commentary, just the numbers.

4

Results Are Tracked Publicly

Every projection, every market, every day is tracked and displayed. No cherry-picking, no hidden losses. Our performance page is updated daily so you can verify the edge yourself.
